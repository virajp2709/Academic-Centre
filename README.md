## 📚 About the Project

Academic Command Center is a centralized dashboard for managing my academic progress across all four years of B.Tech.

The system is designed around the following structure:

B.Tech. 
│
├── 1st Year
│   ├── Semester 1
│   └── Semester 2
│
├── 2nd Year
│   ├── Semester 1
│   └── Semester 2
│
├── 3rd Year
│   ├── Semester 1
│   └── Semester 2
│
└── 4th Year
    ├── Semester 1
    └── Semester 2
